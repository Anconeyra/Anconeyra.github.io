# Surface: portfolio (primary, `/`)

Mode: experience
Platform: web
Audience: technical hiring managers and freelance clients, scanning in under two minutes.
Action: start a conversation (WhatsApp / email).
Proof on hand: 6 real repositories, 15+ Cisco/AWS credentials, real contact channels.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a **security dossier** — a document that is issued, stamped, serialised
and verifiable — refusing both the neon-on-black developer template and the card-grid
portfolio. Security printing is the physical ancestor of the work he does; the page is that
ancestor, working.

OWN-WORLD: Pale sage-grey security paper as the ground, ink blue-black for text, azure
guilloche line-work (parametric rosette + moiré band) as the only ornament, one vermilion
seal red reserved for stamps, the active state, and the single primary action. Record green
means "verified" and nothing else. Archivo for institutional signage scale, JetBrains Mono
for serials, microtext, credentials and the console annex. Hairline rules, perforated edges,
ledger tables, index tabs, a machine-readable footer strip.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor learns the name, the double competence, and that he is
available — then scrolls a document where every claim sits next to its evidence: a
repository, an issuer, a year. It ends at a request form, not a hero button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A dossier cover, full-bleed. A thin top registration strip with the serial
and a live local time. Left column at display scale: the name set in three lines, the role
line beneath it, and a vermilion "DISPONIBLE" seal rotated a few degrees and pressed onto
the sheet with a slight ink offset. Behind the name, the guilloche rosette bleeds off the
right edge at 55% opacity. Below the role, one filled-in request line: a real WhatsApp
control and a real copy-email control — the primary action, in the form's own vocabulary.
Across the foot of the cover, a microtext band at 4px reading the name and role continuously,
and the section index printed as ledger rows. No particle field, no gradient text, no
centred hero.

FORM: Grounded direction 6 of 7 (security document / intaglio). Raised by the field-notebook
discipline of candidate 2: every claim carries an evidence annotation and a chain-of-custody
line, so the document reads like a working file rather than a souvenir. Raised by the
nixie-counter discipline of candidate 1: quantities are stated as physical, counted,
positional registers, never as hero metrics.
